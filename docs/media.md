# Photos and private albums

Open **Media** to upload photos and share private albums with selected guests.

Photos here do not appear on the public wedding website. To place a photo on the public website, use an Image or Hero block under **Page content**.

## Upload photos to the Library

1. Open the **Library** tab.
2. Drag photos into the upload area, or select the area and choose photos from your device.
3. Wait for every upload to finish.

You can upload JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF files. Each file must be 12 MB or smaller. The app prepares large images where possible.

If an upload fails:

- select **Retry** to try again; or
- select **Dismiss** to remove it from the upload list.

Do not close the page while photos are still uploading.

## Create a private album

Private albums are called **Shares** in the app.

1. Open the **Shares** tab.
2. Select **New share**.
3. Enter a clear name, such as “Family album”.
4. Under **Photos**, select **Add photos**.
5. Choose photos from the Library.
6. Under **Guests**, tick the people who should receive the album.
7. Select **Create share**.

Uploading a photo to the Library does not add it to an album automatically.

Each guest can belong to only one photo share. A guest shown as **in another share** must be removed from that share before you can select them here.

## Send an album by email

1. Open the **Shares** tab.
2. Find the album.
3. Select **Email guests**.
4. Read the confirmation.
5. Select **Send emails**.

Each selected guest with an email address receives a private, themed album link. Guests without an email address are skipped.

::: warning Keep album links private
Anyone who receives an album link can open that album. Do not post the link or QR code on a public page or social media.
:::

## Share an album another way

Use one of these options:

- **Copy link** — copies the main album link.
- **Copy** beside a guest's name — copies that guest's personal tracked link.
- **Share QR** — opens your device's share options where supported.
- **Download QR** — saves the QR image so you can attach or print it.

Personal links help the admin connect an open to a selected guest. The album's **open** number counts visits, not unique people.

## Edit an album

1. Find the album under **Shares**.
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Change its name, photos, or guests.
4. Select **Save share**.

To remove a photo from the album, select the **X** on that photo. The photo stays in the Library.

## Delete an album

1. Find the album under **Shares**.
2. Select **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

Guests will no longer be able to open that album. The photos remain in the Library.

## Delete a photo from the Library

1. Open the **Library** tab.
2. Find the photo.
3. Select **Delete**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

This removes the photo from the Library and every private album that uses it.
